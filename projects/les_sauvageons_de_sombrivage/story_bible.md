# Les Sauvageons de Sombrivage — Story & Script Bible (50 Pages)

## Master Story Concept
Ginro, night elf warrior with a two-handed sword, and his friend Yem, dwarf paladin with mace and shield and beautiful plate armor, are wandering in the beautiful night elf forests, on a journey to discover more about themselves, and the world they are in

**Emotional Resonance:** custom_1

## Character Cast & Archetypes
Ginro (A tall, contemplative night elf warrior wielding a massive two-handed greatsword, seeking purpose beyond his ancestral solitude)
Yem (A stout, cheerful dwarf paladin clad in ornate, gleaming plate armor with mace and kite shield, driven by boundless optimism and loyalty)
Pip (A mischievous, bioluminescent clockwork-winged forest sprite who acts as an unpredictable guide)
Elder Miriel (A serene elven stargazer who tends to the ancient celestial shrines hidden beneath the forest canopy)

## The 5 Movements

### Movement I: Under the Canopy of Silver Leaves ()
Ginro and Yem wander beneath the luminous, moon-dappled branches of the great night elf forest, playfully bantering about the contrast between dwarven plate craft and elven swordplay over trail tea. Their quiet reflection is delightfully interrupted when a glowing clockwork sprite named Pip swipes Yem's polished pocket compass, leading them on a whimsical chase through sparkling glades and ancient roots.

### Movement II: The Whisper of Clockwork Roots ()
Following Pip deeper into uncharted groves, the duo discovers an overgrown amphitheater where ancient elven architecture merges with dormant steampunk machinery. While Ginro ponders the forgotten history of his kin, Yem channels his holy light into dormant brass conduits, awakening a garden of humming mechanical flora and celestial star-maps that chart the wider world.

### Movement III: Sparks, Starlight, and Steel ()
The sudden surge of power awakens a slumbering timber-automaton tangled in brambles, which flails in panic and mistakes the companions for intruders. In an exuberant, fluid display of teamwork, Yem guards against falling timber with his radiant kite shield while Ginro's sweeping greatsword cleanly shears away the constricting vines, calming the gentle forest construct without shedding blood.

### Movement IV: Night Fires and Golden Memories ()
Sharing a warm campsite under a shower of falling luminescent leaves, the two companions open up about the personal doubts that spurred their travels. Ginro confesses his fear of drifting aimlessly through eternity, while Yem reveals his wish to protect beauty rather than fight wars, cementing their bond as brothers-in-arms whose differences make them whole.

### Movement V: Toward the Great Horizon ()
Guided by the grateful automaton and Pip, the companions ascend to the edge of the forest canopy atop a towering emerald bluff just as dawn paints the sky in shades of gold and rose. Looking out over an endless expanse of rolling plains and distant spires, Ginro and Yem clasp forearms with bright smiles, stepping eagerly forward into the grand unknown together.
